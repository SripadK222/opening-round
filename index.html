Why Opening-Round

Built for people just starting out.

Opening-Round exists because most resume tools are built for people who already have years of experience to summarize. We built this one for students, first-time job seekers, and anyone whose first resume feels harder than it should.

Five real templates
From clean and traditional to bold and colorful — pick what fits the role you're going for, not just one generic layout.
No experience? No problem
Every part of the builder is designed knowing plenty of great resumes have zero jobs listed — projects and activities count.
Ask Consult AI anything
Stuck on wording, formatting, or what to include? Ask directly and get a real, specific answer — not a generic checklist.
Free to use, no account needed to get started.
